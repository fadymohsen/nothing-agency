- Before an idea has a name, a shape or a campaign plan, there is nothing — a blank space with no inherited answers and no limits. That is where we begin.
- We turn business problems into ideas, ideas into systems, and systems into experiences people remember.
- Strategic thinking, creative direction, design, content, digital and experience under one roof. We help brands find the idea that makes everything else feel inevitable.

## Logo
- **Symbol:** Phi-inspired (mathematical symbol) — open circular form with a diagonal stroke
- **Meaning:** Open space = limitless possibility; diagonal stroke = movement, direction, form
- **Variations:** Horizontal, Vertical, Symbol-integrated into wordmark (replacing the "O")
- **Wordmark font:** Custom lowercase geometric sans-serif with wide letter-spacing

## Pattern System
Three pattern approaches derived from the Phi-inspired symbol:

1. **Diagonal Stroke Grid** — The diagonal stroke repeated horizontally/vertically creating a continuous geometric pattern with architectural brickwork references
2. **Symbol in Motion** — Circle and diagonal stroke separated, alternating rows; effective for motion graphics and transitions
3. **Negative Space** — The negative space of the logo repeated as a simple geometric element for rhythm and texture
4. **Logo Fragment** — The symbol enlarged and cropped to reveal a portion, used as a bold decorative element in corners/edges of layouts
5. **Symbol Repeat** — Full logo symbol repeated as a tiling pattern

## Visual Style Notes
- **Dark mode dominant** — primary presentation uses dark navy backgrounds with gold accents
- **Gold line dividers** — horizontal gold lines used as section separators throughout the identity
- **Brush-stroke texture** — the Phi symbol has an organic, painterly/brush-stroke quality (not perfectly clean)
- **Imagery style** — cinematic, moody, warm golden-hour lighting with dark contrast; architectural and urban scenes
- **Letter-spacing** — wide tracking on the wordmark and subtitle "advertising agency"
- **Whitespace** — generous use of negative space, content left-aligned with breathing room
